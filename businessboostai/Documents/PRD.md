Product Requirements Document (PRD)

Purpose
BusinessBoost AI is an all-in-one mobile and web application designed to help struggling small businesses recover and grow. The app combines AI guidance, business planning, financial tracking, and scheduling into one easy-to-use platform.
Problem Statement
Many small business owners struggle with stress, financial management, and knowing what steps to take to improve their business. Existing tools often solve only one problem instead of providing complete support.
Solution
BusinessBoost AI provides both emotional and practical AI support while helping users organize their finances, create business plans, and stay on schedule.
Target Users
Small business owners
New entrepreneurs
Startups
Local businesses looking to improve operations
Core Features
1. AI Support Chatbot
Provides encouragement and emotional support.
Offers motivation and stress-management tips.
2. AI Business Assistant
Answers business questions.
Creates personalized business plans.
Recommends next steps based on business goals.
Suggests marketing strategies.
Provides sales forecasts and business performance insights.
Gives budgeting and financial recommendations.
Analyzes business progress and identifies areas for improvement.
3. Calendar & Task Manager
Schedule meetings and deadlines.
Set reminders for important tasks.
Track daily and weekly goals.
4. Financial Tracker
Record income and expenses.
Create monthly budgets.
Display spending summaries and financial trends.
Functional Requirements
Secure user login
AI-powered chat interface
Calendar with reminders
Expense and income tracking
Dashboard showing business progress
Secure cloud data storage
Non-Functional Requirements
Simple and intuitive interface
Fast response times
Secure user data
Mobile and desktop compatibility
Reliable AI recommendations
Success Criteria
Users can create a business plan within 10 minutes.
Users consistently track finances and tasks.
Improved organization and budgeting.
Positive user feedback on AI assistance.
